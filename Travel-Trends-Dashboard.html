Project overview

Why this project exists

This dashboard explores how interest in different types of trips in Germany shifts over time – from classic beach holidays to solo, slow and sustainable travel. It turns raw search data into a clear story about emerging travel preferences and gives me a way to show structured analysis, clean visualisation and a focused, niche question inside my portfolio.
