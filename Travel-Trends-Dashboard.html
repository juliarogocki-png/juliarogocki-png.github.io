Portfolio Case Study

Travel Trends Dashboard

How does interest in classic beach holidays shift toward slow travel and solo trips, and which travel types are quietly gaining momentum in Germany?

View mockups View analysis
Focus

Travel demand trends, category comparison and time-based pattern analysis.

Question

Which travel archetypes are stable, seasonal or emerging over time?

Scope

Germany weekly search interest from July 2025 to July 2026.

Project overview

Why this project exists

This project turns raw travel search data into a clear portfolio case study with a niche but understandable question: how travel preferences shift between beach holidays, solo trips, slow travel and sustainable travel.

Analysis

What the data shows

Two views make the story easy to understand: a ranking of average interest and a time-based comparison of the most important travel types.

Average interest ranking

The ranking view compares the overall level of interest for each travel type across the full period. It helps show which categories stay dominant and which ones remain niche but relevant.

  • Beach holidays remain the strongest overall category.
  • Solo travel and slow travel become more visible when looking at change over time.
  • City breaks provide a stable comparison point for short-trip demand.

Weekly trend comparison

This view focuses on movement over time. It makes the seasonal nature of beach holidays visible while showing how solo and sustainable travel gain momentum more gradually.

  • Beach holidays peak strongly in seasonal periods.
  • Solo travel grows from a niche line into a more important signal.
  • Sustainable travel stays lower in volume but points to a longer-term shift in travel behavior.

Key insights

Don't just show the results

This section explains what the numbers mean, not just what they look like.

The dashboard does not only present charts — it helps interpret changing travel behavior over time. Beach holidays remain the most visible category, but the growing presence of solo and slow travel suggests a broader shift toward more personal and flexible trip planning. Instead of showing only final results, this section highlights the meaning behind the patterns and makes the analysis easier to understand in a portfolio context.

Conclusion

What this dashboard adds

This section closes the story with the key takeaway and explains why the analysis matters in the portfolio context.

The dashboard shows that travel interest is not moving in one direction only: beach holidays still lead, but solo and sustainable travel are gaining visibility and create a more nuanced picture of how people want to travel. That makes the project a strong portfolio piece because it combines data analysis, clear storytelling and a clean visual presentation in one focused case study.
