Charts

A cleaner way to present the dashboard

For time-dependent data, trend lines are useful because they show change over time, while a ranking chart gives a compact category comparison. Using both together creates a stronger story than relying on a single chart alone.

Line chart

This chart is the best place to show seasonality, movement across years, and the stronger late growth in some topics. The monthly structure of the CSV makes it a natural time-series view.

View line chart code

Ranking bar chart

The ranking view highlights the long-run comparison more clearly than a single recent snapshot. In this dataset, that is especially useful because several values jump sharply toward the end of the series.

Insights

What the data is actually saying

The CSV shows five monthly keyword series from July 2021 to July 2026. Across that range, beach holiday is frequently the strongest theme, while solo travel becomes much stronger in the later portion of the dataset.
