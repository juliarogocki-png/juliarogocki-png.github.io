About me

Hello!

A selection of projects focused on structure, usability and digital problem-solving. The portfolio combines no-code prototyping, UX thinking and data-driven work.

Background

My background connects analytical work, organization and an interest in digital structures. I enjoy projects where complexity needs to become clearer, more understandable and more useful.

What interests me most is the point where structure and usability meet — whether in a workflow, a prototype, a data-related task or a practical digital concept.

Focus Areas

UX Thinking Digital Structure Workflows Research Support Data Organization Problem-Solving

What matters to me

I like projects that start with a concrete problem and require both structure and empathy. For me, good digital work is not only functional, but also understandable and well organized. I enjoy turning messy requirements and scattered ideas into clear interfaces, clean code, and visual systems that make sense to both users and developers.

This portfolio is still growing, but each page is meant to show how I approach projects: by bringing together clarity, logic, and thoughtful design decisions. Whether it’s a data analysis, a web prototype, or a visual concept, I try to explain the why behind the what—so the work feels intentional, not just finished.
