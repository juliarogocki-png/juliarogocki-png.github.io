Case Study

Tone & Glow

A fitness app prototype that brings workout structure, mobility routines, calorie tracking and quick daily orientation into one connected experience.

Overview

Tone & Glow started with a practical problem from everyday training: important information was spread across different notes and tools, which created friction during the workout itself.

The project goal was to bring structure, clarity and ease of use into one app concept that supports both planning and in-the-moment execution.

App Mockups

Selected prototype screens showing the app structure, workout flow and tracking functions.

Problem

Training plans, exercise notes and calorie tracking were handled in separate places. This interrupted the routine and made the process less intuitive during use.

Goal

The idea was to create one connected workflow that combines workout guidance, flexibility support, calorie tracking and daily orientation.

Approach

I built the first version with Glide as a no-code prototype in order to focus on structure, logic, information architecture and usability.

Core Features

  • Structured training plan with workout splits.
  • Mobility and stretching exercises organized by use case.
  • Calorie tracker with progress-oriented overview.
  • Today view for fast orientation before and during training.
  • Integrated stopwatch for practical workout use.
  • Habit and routine support through connected feature logic.

Data Model & Structure

An important part of the project was translating previously unstructured notes into a clear and connected data model. Different content areas such as training plan, calorie tracking, flexibility exercises and the daily overview were linked into one logical system.

This improved orientation inside the app and helped place information in the context where it was actually needed by the user.

Testing

The prototype was tested in real training situations. This helped reveal which information was useful in the moment and where navigation or labeling still needed improvement.

Iteration

Based on direct use, the structure was refined repeatedly. I adjusted information order, improved clarity and simplified the way users move between the main sections.

Why Glide

Glide made it possible to prototype quickly and validate the concept without starting with full native development.

Limits

The project also showed the limits of no-code tools, especially in interface freedom, visual detail and more advanced interaction ideas.

Next Step

The long-term direction is a rebuild in Swift to gain more control over performance, visual design and interaction behavior.

What I Learned

Tone & Glow showed me how strong everyday pain points can be as a starting point for digital product ideas. It also reinforced that user experience is not only about interface design, but just as much about information structure, logic and usage context.

The project became a practical case study for how I approach systems, usability and iterative improvement.
