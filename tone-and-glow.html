Project

Tone & Glow

A fitness app prototype designed to bring training plans, mobility routines and calorie tracking into one structured and intuitive workflow.

Overview

Tone & Glow started from a practical everyday problem: managing workouts, exercise notes and calorie tracking across different tools created unnecessary breaks in the training routine.

The goal of the project was to create a more seamless experience by combining key workout information, daily guidance and supporting features in one place.

Problem

My training routine involved several media breaks between notes, plans and tracking tools. This made the experience less clear and less efficient in the moment of use.

Goal

The app was meant to bring together workout structure, flexibility exercises, calorie tracking and a natural-feeling navigation in one coherent system.

Approach

I used Glide as a no-code environment to focus first on information architecture, logic, structure and usability before moving to a more advanced implementation.

Core Features

  • Training plan with structured workout splits.
  • Mobility and flexibility exercises organized by problem area.
  • Calorie tracker with daily and target-based progress view.
  • “Today” overview for quick orientation during training.
  • Integrated stopwatch for direct use in workout sessions.

Data Model & Structure

A key part of the project was transforming previously unstructured notes into a clear data model inside Glide. Multiple tables — including training plan, calories, flexibility content, tracker data and the “Today” view — were linked into one connected structure.

This made it possible to place information in a meaningful usage context, so that users could move logically from a routine to the relevant exercise details and understand where they were within the plan.

Testing

I tested the first version directly during real training sessions. This helped identify which information was actually useful in the moment and where the flow still felt unclear.

Iteration

Based on these observations, I adjusted the order of information, improved labels and refined the overview of different sections. The app evolved through repeated practical use rather than a one-time concept phase.

Why Glide

Glide allowed a fast start without writing code and made it possible to focus on data logic, structure and UX decisions early in the process.

Limits

The project also revealed the limits of no-code tools. While Glide was strong for prototyping and structured logic, design freedom and some interaction ideas remained restricted.

Next Step

For that reason, the long-term direction of the project is a future rebuild in Swift in order to gain more control over interface design, performance and interaction.

What I learned

The project showed me that everyday problems can be a strong starting point for digital solutions. It also deepened my understanding that user experience is not only about interface design, but also about data structure, navigation logic and real-life usage context.

Tone & Glow became more than a functional prototype: it developed into a case study that reflects how I think about structure, usability and iterative improvement.
